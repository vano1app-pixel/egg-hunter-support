Egg Hunter

Nothing leaves your phone.

Privacy policy · Effective 29 October 2026

Egg Hunter is developed by Ayush Puri. It does not require an account, and it includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs. There is no Egg Hunter server — the developer does not receive your gameplay, location or activity data.

Location

The hunt plays on the real map around a base you choose, so the app asks for your location while the hunt screen is open. Your saved base coordinate is stored on your phone in UserDefaults. Location is not sent to the developer and is not stored beyond what the current hunt needs. The map itself is drawn by Apple MapKit, and geocoding/search requests go to Apple's services over HTTPS under Apple's privacy terms.

Motion

Optional motion access helps the game judge whether you are standing still. It stays on your device and is not uploaded. The game works without it.

Data stored on your device

Your base coordinate, collected eggs and game progress are stored locally on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes them. There is no account recovery service because there is no account.

Data Not Collected

Egg Hunter's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected". No IDFA, no third-party analytics, no cross-app tracking.

Support and this website

If you email support, your address and message are used to answer the request. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process ordinary connection logs under its privacy statement. This site adds no analytics scripts or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can decline location and motion permissions in iOS Settings and manage them at any time. For privacy questions, email vano1app@gmail.com. You may also contact Ireland's Data Protection Commission.
