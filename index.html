Egg Hunter

Walk an egg home.

A one-player walking game played on the real map around your front door. Set your base, find a crack with an egg in it, and walk it home — while something hatches behind you. Keep moving and it stays back.

Help & support

For questions, problems or feedback, email vano1app@gmail.com.

Playing safely

Egg Hunter is played outdoors on real streets. Stay on pavements, look up from your phone, and never run in traffic. The game asks you to agree to the safety screen once; it still applies every hunt.

Location and motion

The map needs your location while the hunt screen is open. Motion access is optional and makes "standing still" more accurate — the game works without it. Nothing is uploaded; see the privacy policy.

Requirements

iPhone running iOS 17 or later. No account needed. No ads, no chat, nothing leaves your phone except Apple MapKit map tiles.
